Instant Pause

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026 · App com.instantpause.app

In one sentence: Instant Pause collects nothing, sends nothing and has no Internet access. Everything the app knows stays on your phone, in its private storage.

Instant Pause is an Android app that helps you resist impulsive calls. This policy explains, plainly, what data the app uses and what it does not do with it.

1. Data controller

The app's publisher is JANEDOUBY (Instant Pause project, formerly CoolDown).

Contact: janedouby@gmail.com — or, for a public report, github.com/AJANEDOUBY/instant-pause-privacy/issues

As no data is transmitted to the publisher, there is no processing register to consult: the app has no server.

2. Data processed, and only on your device

The app stores locally the information it needs to work:

DataPurposeStorage
Protection code Verify that it is really you before weakening the protection Salted PBKDF2 hash (the code is never stored in plain text)
Time windows Know when to intercept calls Private Room database (cooldown.db)
Allowed contacts Let certain calls through, hung up at 3 minutes Name, international number, displayed number, region
Block log Show you the intercepted calls Number, timestamp, reason — purged after 90 days
Settings Protection state, Zen mode, preferences Private DataStore (cooldown_prefs)

This data is stored in the app's private directory, which other apps cannot read on a non-rooted device.

Allowed contacts

You pick a contact with the Android contact picker. The app does not request the READ_CONTACTS permission: Android only hands it the entry you explicitly selected. The full address book stays out of the app's reach.

The call identifier

To decide whether a call should be intercepted, the app receives the dialled number from Android, at the time of the call, through the Call redirection role you granted. That number is analysed in memory, then kept in the local log described above. It is not transmitted to anyone.

3. What the app does not do

  • No network connection. The app does not declare the INTERNET permission and embeds no HTTP client.
  • No account. No sign-up, no email address, no identifier.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising SDK.
  • No sharing with third parties. No data is sold, rented or transmitted.
  • No advertising, no profiling.
  • No access to your SMS, location, microphone, camera or address book.

4. Permissions requested and why

PermissionWhy
Call redirection role (Telecom)Intercept the outgoing call to show the pause screen
ANSWER_PHONE_CALLSAutomatically hang up at 3 minutes a call to an allowed contact
Device administratorSlow down an impulsive uninstall. No policy is imposed on the device, no remote wipe is possible
SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW / USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENTShow the pause screen over the call screen or the lock screen
POST_NOTIFICATIONS / VIBRATEPersistent protection notification and haptic feedback
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETEDRe-enable protection after a reboot
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARMTrigger windows at the scheduled time
REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONSPrevent Android from putting the protection service to sleep
FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USEKeep protection and the call timer active

Each permission can be revoked in the Android settings. Removing the Call redirection role or the hang-up permission disables the corresponding protection: this is deliberate, the app cannot prevent it.

5. Retention

  • Windows, contacts and settings: until the app is uninstalled.
  • Block log: 90 days maximum, purged automatically.
  • Protection code: hash kept until it is changed or the app is uninstalled.

6. Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and object. As no data is transmitted to the publisher, these rights are exercised directly in the app:

  • Access and rectification: windows, contacts and settings are visible and editable in the app (with the code).
  • Erasure: uninstall the app, or clear its data from Settings → Apps → Instant Pause → Storage. Everything disappears with the private storage.
  • The anti-uninstall protection prevents an impulsive uninstall: first deactivate the device administrator in Settings → Security → Device administration, with your code. It is a guardrail against yourself, not data retention.

Legal basis: performance of the service you request (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). No processing takes place without your action.

7. Security

The code is kept as a PBKDF2 hash with a random salt and constant-time comparison. Data resides in the Android sandbox. The app contains no API key, no server secret, and therefore nothing to steal remotely.

8. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 and collects no data about them.

9. Changes

Any change to this policy will be published at this address and dated at the top of the document. If a future version introduced data processing (for example optional synchronisation), it would state so explicitly before activation.
